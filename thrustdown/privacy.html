Privacy policy

Thrustdown: Rocket Lander for Android, by rafmanbro. Effective October 5, 2026.

This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game itself does not collect anything about you. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, best times and settings are saved on your phone only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game has no accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device model, operating system and app version;
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

This information goes to Google, not to us. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game settings. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings.

Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" inside the game. The payment is processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can switch the ads off.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please write to the developer e-mail address shown on the game's Google Play page.
